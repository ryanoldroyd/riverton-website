Meet the Doctors

Experience you can feel from the first hello.

Two doctors with deep training, a love of teaching, and a shared commitment to patient-first care.

Dr. Marilyn Sadler, DDS

Doctor of Dental Surgery

Meet Dr. Sadler, our wonderful third-generation dentist. With an incredibly diverse background, she has experienced every role in a dental office — from dental assistant to front office staff, hygienist, and now dentist. Her journey has given her a great appreciation for teamwork, and she truly values her staff.

Originally from North Dakota, Dr. Sadler's passion for dentistry blossomed while working as a dental assistant in rural Alaska. Inspired by her experiences there, she decided to become a dentist. Before earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Utah, she taught as a dental assistant instructor and served as an adjunct dental hygiene instructor at Weber State University.

Dr. Sadler's commitment to her profession goes hand in hand with her love for giving back. After completing her dental hygiene education, she took on leadership roles in the Northern Utah Dental Hygiene Association, serving as President for two years. She has also been the liaison for dental schools with the Utah Dental Association and is actively involved on the DOPL dental board.

Her generosity extends beyond local efforts — Dr. Sadler enjoys participating in humanitarian trips. She has traveled to the Dominican Republic with the G3 Foundation, worked in Kenya with 100 Humanitarians, and Honduras and Mexico with Global Dental Partners, where she provided dental care and taught valuable life skills. She also dedicates time to serve at Midvale CBC, a local community clinic, helping those in need year-round.

On a personal note, Dr. Sadler met her husband, Trevor, in Alaska, and they have happily called Utah home for over 21 years. Together, they have two adventurous children, McKinley and Summit, along with a sweet old golden retriever named Teton.

Dr. Ayefa Durrani

General Dentist · Periodontics Focus

Dr. Durrani prides herself on providing comprehensive dental care and creating a relationship of trust with her patients.

Dr. Durrani studied in a three-year program leading to a Master of Dental Surgery (Periodontics) at the National University of Singapore, and earned a Membership of Restorative Dentistry from the Royal College of Surgeons in Edinburgh, UK in 2010. After practicing primarily as a periodontist for several years, she moved to the United States and completed the Advanced Education in General Dentistry program at Marquette University School of Dentistry in 2013.

That training led to a career in the U.S. as a general dentist with a strong interest in periodontics. In addition to all aspects of general dentistry, her areas of expertise include managing severe gum disease, implants, gum recession, and crown lengthening surgery. Outside the office, Dr. Durrani loves spending time with her family and traveling.
